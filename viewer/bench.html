nolli · layout stability

Every box of a dataset's default cut is unfolded once, and every open container folded once, each starting from the default cut; the layout before and after is compared on the boxes present in both. order: share of left/right and above/below relations between pairs of boxes that survive (1 = all). knn: share of each box's 3 nearest boxes that stay its 3 nearest (1 = all). residual: movement left after the best shift and uniform scale, as a share of the picture's diagonal (0 = none). edges stacked: in the default cut, the share of edge length drawn on top of another edge (0 = none). Parameters: ?d=name,name datasets, &engines=elk,fcose.
